The HVAC Team Grapevine Homeowners Rely On

Many homeowners only think about their HVAC system when it stops working, and by then comfort is already gone. Grapevine HVAC takes a different approach. We get there quickly, identify the failed part, explain the problem in plain language, and complete the fix with the right parts on hand.

Whether you're dealing with a condenser that trips its breaker, a heat pump stuck in defrost, or a mini-split flashing error codes, our experienced technicians have the training and parts to handle just about any household HVAC challenge.

  • AC repair and replacement
  • Furnace and heating system service

No Surprises

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Before we start any repair, we find out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check the age and tonnage of your system, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get a straightforward recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a worn capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear slowly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, schedule an inspection right away. Catching it early often keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

Not every visit is about a breakdown. Plenty of Grapevine homeowners ask us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, a replacement on your own timeline beats a rushed swap in the middle of a heat wave. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your equipment has left.

Preventive Care

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the idea behind our maintenance plans. Twice a year, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. This is especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a regular maintenance schedule cuts operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Sized for Your Home

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

An undersized system runs nonstop and never catches up on the hottest days, while an oversized one short-cycles, wastes energy, and leaves the house humid. Grapevine HVAC sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual home. That means a proper load calculation that accounts for square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout, rather than simply matching the tonnage of the old unit. For ductless mini-splits, it means matching head capacity to each zone so every room holds its setpoint.

Homes change over time. An addition or a finished basement can outgrow the single-stage system that once kept up just fine. Today's two-stage and variable-speed equipment holds tighter temperatures and uses much less energy. If you're torn between a heat pump and a furnace-and-AC pair, or ducted and ductless, we'll lay out the real trade-offs in SEER2 ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

The HVAC Repairs Grapevine Homes Need Most

The majority of repair calls we get in Grapevine come down to a handful of recurring problems. The AC runs but the air never gets cold. The furnace won't light, or it lights and shuts off minutes later. The evaporator coil freezes solid, the condenser hums without starting, or a ductless head flashes an error code. Add refrigerant leaks, clogged drains, and forgetful thermostats, and you have the full list. Our technicians handle all of them, starting with the exact part that failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. We come prepared to replace capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
